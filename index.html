🕯️ Candlestick Patterns Visual Dictionary

Bullish Engulfing

Reversal (Bull)

A large teal candle completely swallows the prior orange candle body. Signals violent buyer takeover at key support.

Hammer (Pin Bar)

Reversal (Bull)

Small upper body with an elongated lower wick at least twice the body height. Shows sellers tried to push price down but buyers aggressively absorbed them.

Morning Star

3-Bar Bullish

A 3-candle reversal: long orange candle, small gapped indecision star, followed by a strong teal candle piercing deeply above the midpoint.

Shooting Star

Reversal (Bear)

Appears at the top of an uptrend. Long upper wick shows buyers drove price up, but sellers completely took control and drove the close down.

Bearish Engulfing

Reversal (Bear)

A large orange candle overwhelms and covers the previous teal candle body. High probability exhaustion signal.

Doji

Equilibrium / Pause

Open and close prices are virtually identical. Indicates a tug-of-war where neither buyers nor sellers hold dominance.

Part 1 • The Analytical Framework

In-Depth Trading Analysis Breakdown

3 Confluence Pillars
📊

1. Technical & Price Action Analysis

Technical analysis evaluates price charts to gauge supply and demand imbalances.

  • • Market Structure (BOS & CHoCH): An uptrend creates series of Higher Highs (HH) and Higher Lows (HL). A break below a prior Higher Low signals a Change of Character (CHoCH) or reversal.
  • • Multi-Timeframe Alignment: Always look at the Daily/1-Hour chart to confirm the macro trend. Only take lower timeframe (5M/15M) trades aligned with the higher timeframe momentum.
  • • The Confluence Secret: Never enter on a single signal. Enter when Supertrend is Bullish + price tests the EMA 20 + MACD confirms a fresh bullish cross.
High Confluence = High Win Rate
🌐

2. Fundamental & Macro Catalysts

Fundamentals dictate the long-term tide; technicals dictate your exact entry and exit timing.

  • • Central Bank Interest Rates: When central banks (Federal Reserve, RBI) cut interest rates, liquidity expands, sending stocks & crypto soaring. Rate hikes cause contractions.
  • • Economic News Calendars: High-impact events (CPI Inflation, Non-Farm Payrolls, GDP) create huge sudden spikes. Beginners should avoid entering right at news release times.
  • • Crypto On-Chain Flow: Large Bitcoin outflows from exchanges into cold storage indicate institutional accumulation (Bullish), while massive exchange inflows indicate sell-offs (Bearish).
Follow The Global Liquidity Tide
⚡

3. Order Flow & Liquidity Sweeps

Markets are auction mechanisms moving towards areas of highest resting orders.

  • • Depth of Market (DOM): The Order Book displays limit bids (support) and limit asks (resistance). When asks are heavy, price requires enormous buying volume to break through.
  • • Liquidity Sweeps (Stop Hunts): Retail traders place stop-losses just under obvious swing lows. Institutional algorithms trigger fake breakdowns to harvest that liquidity before rallying!
  • • Fair Value Gaps (FVG): Violent, fast single-direction candle thrusts leave unfilled liquidity zones that market prices frequently retrace to retest.
Think Like An Institutional Market Maker

Long Lower Wick: Price dropped significantly, but aggressive buyers stepped in and pushed it all the way back up. Strong bullish rejection!
Long Upper Wick: Price rallied, but sellers slammed it down before the candle closed. Strong bearish rejection!

📈 How to Identify Market Trends

Structure Rules

A trend is your greatest ally. Never fight the dominant direction of the market.

1. Uptrend (Bull)

Price forms Higher Highs (HH) & Higher Lows (HL). Look ONLY for Long / Buy setups.

2. Downtrend (Bear)

Price forms Lower Highs (LH) & Lower Lows (LL). Look ONLY for Short / Sell setups.

3. Range (Choppy)

Price bounces between support & resistance. Trade the boundaries or wait for breakouts.

⚠️ Breakouts vs. Fakeouts (Bull & Bear Traps)

Crucial Lesson

Amateur traders buy the moment price crosses above resistance, only to get trapped when price immediately dumps back into the channel.

✅ True Breakout: Candle body closes completely outside the resistance line with above-average volume. Price then retests previous resistance as new support.
❌ Fakeout (Trap): Only a long thin wick pokes above resistance while the candle body closes back below it. High likelihood of rapid dump!

Part 3 • Mathematical Foundations

How to Calculate Every Formula in Trading

No More Guessing
FORMULA 1 Risk Management

Exact Position Sizing (Units)

Units = (Account Capital × Risk %) / |Entry - StopLoss|

Real Example: You have $100,000. You risk 1% ($1,000). Entry is $90,000 and Stop-Loss is $88,000 ($2,000 distance).

Units = $1,000 / $2,000 = 0.50 BTC
If SL hits, you lose strictly $1,000 (1%).
FORMULA 2 Profit Expectancy

Risk-to-Reward Ratio (R:R)

R:R = |Target Price - Entry| / |Entry - StopLoss|

Real Example: Entry is $90,000, Stop-Loss is $88,000 ($2,000 risk), and Target is $96,000 ($6,000 reward).

R:R = $6,000 / $2,000 = 1 : 3.0
With 1:3 R:R, a 30% win rate is highly profitable!
FORMULA 3 Margin Math

Margin Required & Leverage

Margin = (Total Position Value) / Leverage

Real Example: Buying 1 Bitcoin at $90,000 with 10x Leverage.

Margin = $90,000 / 10 = $9,000 Collateral
You control $90k with only $9k cash down.
FORMULA 4 Survival Math

Estimated Liquidation Price

Liq Price = Entry × (1 - (0.90 / Leverage))

Notice: At 50x leverage, a mere 1.8% drop triggers total account liquidation! At 2x leverage, it takes a 45% crash.

Golden Rule: Keep leverage below 5x for safety.
FORMULA 5 Harmonic Math

Fibonacci Golden Pocket

Golden Level = Low + ((High - Low) × 0.618)

In nature and markets, 61.8% represents natural geometric equilibrium. Retracements into this pocket frequently provide the highest win rate reversals.

FORMULA 6 Derivatives

Options Breakeven Price

Call BE = Strike + Premium Paid
Put BE = Strike - Premium Paid

Example: Buy $90,000 Call for $2,500 premium. Price must rise above $92,500 at expiry for net profit.
